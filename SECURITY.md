# Security policy

## Reporting a problem

Email security@jarrettd.com. The same contact is in the site's
[security.txt](https://jarrettd.com/.well-known/security.txt). Please include what you found, how to
reproduce it and, if you can, what you think the impact is. I will reply to say it arrived and keep
you updated until it is fixed or I have explained why it is not a problem.

## What is in scope

- The site at jarrettd.com, which is static files: the headers it sends, the Content Security Policy,
  and anything in the built pages.
- This repository: the workflows, the scripts that check the build, and the dependencies.

## What is not

- Denial of service and volumetric testing.
- Social engineering of me or of the hosting provider.
- Findings that need access to an account that is not yours.

## Supported versions

Only the current site, at the head of the `main` branch.
